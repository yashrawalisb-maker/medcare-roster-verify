Why it works this way

  • Evidence first. The practice is asked only when plan-side signals (claims, member reports) point to a change.

  • A reward that matters. Answering pauses the plan’s calls for this verification cycle.
  • Safe by design. A practice ID check comes first, payment details are never editable here, and nothing is removed on a prediction.
